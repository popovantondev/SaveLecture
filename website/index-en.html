From a meeting to an organized archive

01

Text with time and speaker

Find the relevant part: timestamps and highlighted names help you navigate a long lecture.

02

Slides beside the transcript

Compare the explanation with the shared material. The main slide stays complete; thumbnails help you find a moment.

03

Pause, finish and recover

Pause collection, resume the same lecture or finish it. After a crash, the previous file is retained.

Flexible settings

Adapt it to your lecture

Adjust similarity, stability, allowed shift and check interval separately. Plain explanations help you choose between fewer repeats and a more detailed archive.
